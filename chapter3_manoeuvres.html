The OSM Safety Routine

Every manoeuvre — without exception — must follow Observar → Señalizar → Maniobrar.

⚠️ Signalling NEVER confers right of way (Art. 109 RGC)

The indicator only informs other road users of your intention. Penalty for not signalling: 200 € (grave infraction).

Step 1 — Observe (Obligatory mirror sequence)

1. Interior mirror
Check what is behind you in the centre mirror first.
2. Exterior mirror
Mirror on the side of the manoeuvre.
3. Blind spot
Visible head-turn of ~45°. A mere eye movement is not enough. Motorcycles and cyclists hide here.

Joining Traffic — Incorporación (Art. 72–73 RGC)

Applies when moving off from outside the traffic flow. Resuming after a red light is NOT classed as joining traffic.

You must ALWAYS yield to all existing traffic

From private roads, garages, petrol stations, rest areas: drive at speed allowing immediate stop; give way to ALL users from both directions.

Acceleration lane — 3-step rule

1
Verify at the START of the lane that you pose no danger. Be prepared to STOP if necessary.
2
Give way to all vehicles in the lane you wish to join.
3
Accelerate to reach suitable speed at the END of the lane.

Bus Rule — Art. 73.2 RGC

Urban area bus leaving a signalled stop

Other drivers must: pull over laterally if possible; slow down smoothly; or STOP if necessary. Conditions: regular-service bus + signalled stop + indicator on + inside built-up area. The bus must still signal and ensure safety — courtesy is "in the measure of the possible".

Priority rule — Art. 74.2

Overtaking — Adelantamiento (Arts. 82–89 RGC)
⚠️ 2022 Change: +20 km/h margin ABOLISHED

Since March 2022 (Ley 18/2021), you can NEVER exceed the speed limit to overtake — under any circumstances.

General rules

RuleDetail
Which side to overtakeLEFT — always, as general rule
Speed requiredNoticeably superior to overtaken vehicle (but never exceeding speed limit)
1.5 metre ruleWhen overtaking pedestrians, animals, two-wheeled vehicles, immobilised vehicles: min 1.5 m lateral clearance + partial or complete lane change. Complete lane change mandatory on multi-lane roads.
If a problem developsSlow down immediately and return behind original vehicle using right indicator

Overtaking on the RIGHT — permitted exceptions

  • Vehicle ahead signals left turn or stop on the LEFT side
  • Urban roads with 2+ lanes same direction (bordered by markings)
  • Trams running at the centre of a two-way road
  • Three-lane two-way road: centre lane only (when free of oncoming traffic)

Overtaken vehicle obligations (Art. 86)

  • Stick to the right kerb — without invading the shoulder
  • Must NOT increase speed
  • Slow down if danger arises mid-overtake
  • Forbidden: increase speed or perform manoeuvres making overtaking dangerous
Where Overtaking is Forbidden — Art. 87 RGC
R-305 sign
Forbids overtaking all motor vehicles (not two-wheeled motorbikes) without invading opposite lane. 200 € + 4 puntos.
Continuous centre line
Cannot be crossed, trodden on or driven to the left of. R-305 prevails over a broken line.
Also forbidden without signs
  • Bends and crests with reduced visibility
  • Level crossings and their proximity
  • Pedestrian crossings (unless slow enough to stop)
  • Cycle lane junctions
  • Tunnels with ONE lane per direction
  • Junctions (exceptions: roundabouts, priority junctions, two-wheelers)
✓ Special case — Art. 88–89

Even with R-305 or continuous line, the opposite lane MAY be invaded to pass slow/fragile users (cyclists, mopeds, pedestrians, animals, agricultural machinery) or stationary obstacles — if: no oncoming traffic; appropriate signalling; no danger to oncoming cyclists; 1.5 m clearance maintained.

Changing Direction — Giro (Arts. 74–77 RGC)

Turning LEFT — position before turning

Two-way road with 3 lanes (broken lines)CENTRAL lane
One-way road with 3 lanesLEFTMOST lane
Cycles & mopeds turning left on interurban two-way roads

Must position on the RIGHT SHOULDER and perform a two-stage turn from there. This rule does NOT apply on urban roads.

Exit ramps — deceleration lane (Art. 77)

  • Signal well in advance and move to rightmost lane
  • Enter the deceleration lane as early as possible
  • Speed reduction must happen INSIDE the deceleration lane — NEVER on the main carriageway

When changing direction is FORBIDDEN

  • R-302 (no right turn) or R-303 (no left turn — also forbids U-turn) sign displayed
  • Continuous longitudinal lines would have to be crossed
  • Visibility insufficient for a left turn
  • Without signalling in advance
U-Turn — Cambio de Sentido (Arts. 78–79 RGC)
Definition

180° inversion of direction, completed in a SINGLE forward manoeuvre. Using reverse gear to complete it is NOT permitted.

Where U-turns are FORBIDDEN (Art. 79)

  • R-304 (no U-turn) or R-303 (no left turn) sign
  • Road markings forbid crossing
  • Bends, crests with reduced visibility
  • Level crossings
  • Tunnels and underpasses (pasos inferiores)
  • Pedestrian crossings
  • Junctions and their vicinity (except roundabouts)
  • Autopistas and autovías (except at designated openings)
  • All stretches where overtaking is forbidden
⚠️ KEY TRAP — paso superior vs paso inferior

A flyover (paso superior) is NOT in the forbidden list — U-turns there are ALLOWED if safety conditions are met. Only an underpass (paso inferior) and tunnels are forbidden. Do NOT confuse them!

Reversing — Marcha Atrás (Arts. 80–81 RGC)
General rule: FORBIDDEN on all roads

Driving in reverse gear is forbidden on all roads as a normal mode of circulation. Only permitted in two specific situations.

Complementary manoeuvre
As part of parking, joining traffic, or leaving a space. Maximum distance: 15 metres. Cannot invade an intersection.
Dead-end / impossible forward movement
Roads where turning is impossible. 15 m limit does NOT apply. May invade a junction if strictly necessary.
⛔ Absolute prohibitions

Reversing on autopistas and autovías: absolutely FORBIDDEN even if you missed your exit. Continue to the next exit. Penalty: up to 500 € + 6 puntos (muy grave).

Reversing signal when reverse light fails

Arm signal
Arm extended horizontally with the palm FACING BACK.
