Seat Belts — Obligations & Exemptions
General rule: mandatory on BOTH urban AND interurban roads

All drivers and passengers must wear properly fastened authorised seat belts. Penalty: 200 € + 3 puntos.

Seat belt exemptions — ONLY inside urban areas (NOT on motorways, dual carriageways, or travesías)

  • Drivers reversing or parking
  • Drivers with a medical exemption certificate
  • Goods delivery handlers on short distances for loading/unloading
  • Drivers and passengers of emergency response vehicles
  • Taxi drivers on duty in built-up areas
  • Persons under 135 cm tall in a REAR seat (must use child restraint instead)
  • Instructors/examiners responsible for additional vehicle controls during tuition or test
⛔ Key exam trap — on motorways, dual carriageways and travesías:

ALL exemptions cease. Taxi drivers, delivery handlers, emergency vehicle passengers — EVERYONE must wear the belt on these roads, no exceptions.

Child Restraint Systems — Under 135 cm
General rule

Children under 135 cm of age ≥ 3 years must use an authorised child restraint system adapted to their size and weight — in the REAR SEAT.

Exceptional cases to allow front seat

  • Vehicle has NO rear seats
  • ALL rear seats are already occupied by minors under 135 cm
  • It is not possible to install all child restraint systems in the rear seats
⛔ Rear-facing child seat + front airbag

If the child is in the front seat in a REAR-FACING child restraint and there is a front passenger airbag — the airbag MUST be DEACTIVATED. A deployed airbag against a rear-facing child can cause fatal injuries.

Child under 3 years oldMUST use child restraint system — NO exceptions. Cannot use regular seat belt.
Child 3+ years under 135 cm — no child seat availableUse the appropriate regular safety belt
Who is responsible for child restraintThe DRIVER — regardless of whether they are the child's parent. 200 € + 3 puntos.
Forward-facing seat + active front airbagPermitted — airbag deactivation only required for REAR-FACING seats

Goods & Load Transportation

Loading/unloading rules

  • Must take place OFF the road as a general rule
  • If on the road is necessary: take place on the side nearest to the kerb
  • Immobilise the vehicle properly
  • STRICTLY FORBIDDEN to deposit goods on road, hard shoulder or pedestrian areas
  • Complete in as little time as possible

Load accommodation — what the load must NOT do

  • Compromise vehicle stability
  • Obscure lighting, number plates, or driver signals
  • Become prone to dragging, falling off, or moving dangerously
  • Produce noise, dust or inconveniences (dusty/at-risk loads must be COMPLETELY covered)

Motorcycles & mopeds towing trailers

Max trailer MAMMust NOT exceed 50% of the motorcycle's unladen weight
VisibilityDaytime only, conditions that do not compromise visibility
Speed reductionReduce by approximately 10% of generic speed limit on interurban roads
Passengers in trailerNEVER allowed — under any circumstances whatsoever
