ITV Results

✓ FAVOURABLE
Fit for circulation. Slight defects (DL): repair within 2 months, no new inspection needed. V-19 sticker issued — upper RIGHT corner interior windshield.
⚠ UNFAVOURABLE
Serious defects (DG). Vehicle disabled EXCEPT to travel to workshop or return to ITV station within 2 months from first unfavourable result.
✗ FAILED
Very serious defects (DMG). Vehicle disabled — must be transported by crane. Once repaired, must be re-inspected within 2 months.

Infractions Implying Loss of Points

3 Points Loss — Serious offences

  • Changing direction against Traffic Law dispositions
  • Using audio headphones/headsets that reduce attention — or manually using mobile phone, browser, communication systems while driving
  • Driving vehicles fitted with radar detection mechanisms or kinemometers

4 Points Loss — Serious or very serious offences

  • Driving without a permit/licence for the category of vehicle
  • Not respecting right of way; not stopping at STOP/GIVE WAY; ignoring red traffic light
  • Breaking overtaking rules; hazard/obstructing oncoming; overtaking in low visibility
  • Reversing on motorways and dual carriageways
  • Ignoring Traffic Officer's signals
  • Not maintaining safe distance from preceding vehicle
  • Not using/improperly using seat belt, child restraints, helmet and mandatory protection
  • Drink driving: expired air 0.25–0.50 mg/l (general) / 0.15–0.30 mg/l (new/professional)

6 Points Loss — Very serious offences

  • Drink driving — TWICE the limit: expired air >0.50 mg/l (general) / >0.30 mg/l (new/professional)
  • Driving under influence of drugs, stimulants, or other identical substances
  • Refusing to undergo alcohol/drug tests
  • Reckless driving; contra-flow driving; non-authorised races or competitions
  • Devices to ELUDE traffic police controls or radar speed trap detectors (jammers)
  • Using a HAND-HELD mobile phone device while driving
  • Throwing objects on road or vicinity that could cause fires or accidents
  • Overtaking endangering cyclists or without leaving minimum 1.5 m separation
